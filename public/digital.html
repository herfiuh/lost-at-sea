STATION RULE:
GPS latitude above 25.17° N → continue on a SOUTH track.

🧠 Why does the number fall?

Latitude is measured from the Equator. Moving north makes the latitude number larger; moving south makes it smaller.

29.20° N
→
27.00° N
→
25.17° N

Smaller latitude = farther SOUTH.

🔊 2. Bathymetric Sonar Profile

Sonar uses sound to measure the water beneath the vessel.

A sudden decrease in depth means the bottom is rising toward the vessel.
STATION RULE:
Depth drops below 5 m → EMERGENCY TURN.
