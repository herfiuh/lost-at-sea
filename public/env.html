ENVIRONMENTAL DIVISION 01 / 04
Environmental Training

💨 + 🌊 Read the Sea

💨 1. Shamal Crosswind

A crosswind can push a vessel sideways.

In the simulation: NW wind pushes the vessel EAST. Counter it by steering WEST into the wind.
STATION RULE:
NW Shamal → pushes vessel EAST → steer slightly WEST into the wind → maintain SOUTH course.

🌊 2. Wave Swell Tacking

Waves hitting the vessel broadside can cause severe rolling.

Instead: meet the incoming swell at approximately 45°
STATION RULE:
Waves at 90° sideways → severe rolling.
Tack at approximately 45° to slice through incoming swells.
💨 WIND = COURSE CORRECTION     🌊 WAVES = HULL SAFETY

💨 Challenge 1 — The Shamal

💨 NW WIND → 🚢 → EAST DRIFT

Ask:

“Which direction is the wind coming from?”
“Which way will it push the vessel?”
“Which way should we steer to compensate?”

Students should discover: NW wind → EAST drift → steer WEST into the wind.

🌊 Challenge 2 — The Waves

🌊 → 🚢 ← 🌊

Ask:

“What happens if waves hit the vessel directly from the side?”
“What angle does our rule recommend?”

Students should discover: 90° = severe rolling → approximately 45° is safer.

🧠 Challenge 3 — Explain the difference

WIND

Pushes us off course.

→ CORRECT THE HEADING
WAVES

Affect hull stability.

→ CHANGE THE ANGLE

Different problem. Different response.
